Reading the board

Implied value is what a token would be worth if the protocol's record stayed as it is: its TVL priced at the median mcap/TVL of its traded peers, adjusted for momentum, track record and chain breadth. It is this board's claim, not a prediction that a token will be issued.

Band is the peer set's interquartile range through the same adjustments. Confidence scores the evidence behind the number. Readiness is a separate signal and never moves the value.
